B2B & Wholesale Fulfillment in Houston

Retail and wholesale orders play by different rules. We handle EDI-ready pallet shipments, routing guides, ASN labels and retailer compliance so your bulk POs reach big-box and distributor DCs chargeback-free.

Next Step 3PL fulfills B2B and wholesale orders from Houston for brands shipping to retailers, distributors and other businesses — not just consumers. Bulk purchase orders, pallet and case quantities, and strict retailer routing guides are a different discipline from DTC, and we run them to compliance so your invoices get paid in full.

Whether you're supplying a big-box chain, stocking a distributor's DC, or shipping wholesale to other brands, we handle the labeling, documentation and carrier routing that keep your accounts chargeback-free — while the same building also fulfills your direct-to-consumer orders.

What B2B fulfillment covers

We handle the parts of wholesale that trip up DTC-only 3PLs:

  • Case- and pallet-pick for bulk and wholesale orders
  • EDI-ready processing, ASN (856) generation and PO management
  • Retailer routing-guide compliance and carrier selection
  • GS1-128 (UCC-128) pallet and carton labeling
  • Palletizing, shrink-wrapping and LTL / full-truckload freight scheduling
  • Retail and distributor compliance to avoid chargebacks

Retail compliance, done right

Big-box retailers and distributors enforce routing guides to the letter — the wrong label placement, a missing ASN, or a booking outside the delivery window can trigger a chargeback that erases the margin on the order. We build each shipment to the retailer's spec: correct labels, accurate advance ship notices, and freight booked to the required carrier and window.

B2B and DTC under one roof

Most growing brands sell both wholesale and direct, and splitting that across two providers means two inventory pools and two bills. We hold one pool of your stock in Houston and fulfill both channels from it — a pallet to a retailer's DC and a single unit to a shopper's door — with port-direct receiving for the imported inventory that feeds both.
