Container Drayage & Devanning in Houston

The advantage no inland 3PL can offer. Your import container clears the Port of Houston and is drayed straight to our dock — unloaded, counted, and shelved the same day.

Get a Fulfillment Quote →Calculate My Costs

Next Step 3PL is a port-direct 3PL: our warehouse sits close enough to the Port of Houston that we can arrange drayage, devan your container, and have your goods on the shelf the same day it clears. For importers, that removes the single most expensive and slowest part of the traditional model — trucking a container inland to a distant fulfillment center before anyone even opens the doors.

The Port of Houston is the United States' leading port for foreign waterborne tonnage, which means sailing schedules, chassis and drayage capacity are deep here in a way they simply aren't for inland 3PLs.

What port-direct handling includes

From the moment your container is available at the terminal, we own the chain:

  • Drayage scheduling and chassis from the Port of Houston to our dock
  • Live or drop devanning of floor-loaded or palletized containers
  • Full piece count and condition inspection on unload
  • Damage and shortage documentation with photos
  • Immediate putaway to storage, or direct routing to FBA prep and fulfillment

Same-day devanning

Speed at the dock protects the rest of your supply chain. Because we schedule drayage around the terminal's availability and devan on arrival, your inventory is counted and sellable the same day rather than sitting on a chassis accruing per-diem and detention charges. That's the difference between shipping DTC orders within 48 hours of a vessel clearing and waiting a week for an inland handoff.

Straight into fulfillment

Because devanning, storage and fulfillment all happen in the same building, there's no transfer between vendors. We can shelve your goods, prep a portion for Amazon FBA, and start shipping DTC orders — all from the container we just unloaded.
