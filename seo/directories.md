# Directory listings & citations

Local ranking depends heavily on **citations** — other sites listing your business
name, address and phone number. What matters is not volume but *consistency*: the
NAP must match `business.json` character for character everywhere. "Suite G2" on
one listing and "Ste G2" on another are read as two different businesses, and the
signal splits.

**Copy the exact values from [`business.json`](business.json). Do not retype them.**

---

## Who does what

I can prepare everything — the exact text to paste, the category choices, the
descriptions at the right length, and this tracker. **I cannot submit these for
you.** Directory submissions require creating accounts, accepting terms, and
sometimes phone or postcard verification tied to your identity. Those are yours
to do, and you should not hand the credentials to an automated process either.

The realistic split: 10–15 minutes each, done once, mostly permanent.

## After you list

1. Record the live listing URL next to the row so it can be audited later.
2. Change the ☐ to ☑.
3. If a listing shows a wrong address or an old phone number, fix it at the source
   rather than creating a second listing — duplicates actively hurt.

## Reviews

Citations get you into the map pack; reviews decide your position within it. The
single highest-value ask is a Google review from a happy client that mentions the
service and the city in their own words ("container devanning in Houston"). Ask
after a successful peak season or a first smooth container, not at random.
