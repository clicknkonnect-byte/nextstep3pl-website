E-commerce Order Fulfillment in Houston

When an order lands in your store, the clock starts. We pick, pack, and ship it the same or next business day from our Houston warehouse — with live tracking synced straight back to your customer.

Next Step 3PL runs outsourced e-commerce fulfillment for growing brands out of a single Houston warehouse minutes from the Port of Houston. Your inventory lives on our racks, your store connects to our system, and every order that comes in is picked, packed, and out the door the same or next business day — usually before a 2 PM cut-off.

Because we ship from the center of the country, the average parcel leaves in a lower shipping zone. That means 60% of the US population is reachable in one to two days by ground, without you paying for a second coast-to-coast warehouse.

What's included

Full-service fulfillment means you hand off the whole physical operation and keep the visibility. Every plan includes:

  • Inbound receiving, counting and putaway of your inventory
  • Real-time order sync from Shopify, Amazon, TikTok Shop, WooCommerce, eBay and 120+ platforms
  • Same or next-business-day pick and pack (2 PM cut-off)
  • Plain or branded packaging, inserts and custom kitting on request
  • Discounted multi-carrier rates (USPS, UPS, FedEx) chosen by cost and speed
  • Tracking numbers written back to your store automatically
  • A live inventory portal with low-stock alerts and itemized billing

How onboarding works

Most brands are shipping from Houston within a week. You connect your store so orders flow in automatically, ship us your stock (or route an import container straight from the port), and we receive and shelve it. From there, every order that hits your store is picked and shipped on our floor while you watch it move in real time.

Why fulfill from Houston

Houston is the quiet winner of US fulfillment geography. It sits on three interstates (I-10, I-45 and 610), hosts the nation's leading port for foreign waterborne tonnage, and carries no Texas state income tax — which keeps both your landed cost and our operating cost below coastal 3PL hubs. For importers especially, having fulfillment and port-direct devanning under one roof removes an entire inland freight leg.
