Kitting & Assembly in Houston

Subscription boxes, gift sets, multi-SKU bundles, retail displays — we assemble them by hand to your spec so they ship as one clean, ready-to-sell unit.

Get a Fulfillment Quote →Calculate My Costs

Next Step 3PL provides kitting and assembly in Houston for brands that sell more than a single loose item. When several components need to become one sellable unit — a subscription box, a gift set, a retail display, a promotional bundle — we build it by hand to your exact spec and store it ready to ship.

Kitting lives in the same building as your storage and fulfillment, so components come off the shelf, get assembled, and go back as finished goods without leaving the warehouse or waiting on a second vendor.

What we assemble

If it needs to arrive as one unit, we can build it:

  • Subscription boxes on a recurring cycle
  • Gift sets and multi-SKU bundles
  • Retail and display-ready packs for wholesale
  • Promotional inserts, samples and marketing collateral
  • Shrink-wrapping, poly-bagging and light assembly
  • Quality checks on every finished unit

Ready for launches and seasons

Kitting demand tends to spike — a product launch, a holiday gift set, a subscription drop. Because we scale labor to the build, we can turn around large kitting runs on a deadline and then hold the finished units as sellable inventory, so the moment an order comes in it ships as a single, clean SKU.

One SKU out the door

Once a kit is built, it behaves like any other item in your catalog: it's counted in your portal, picked as one unit, and shipped same or next day. That keeps your fulfillment simple even when the product inside is complex.
